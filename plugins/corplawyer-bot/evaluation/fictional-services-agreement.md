# Fictional services agreement — exercise only

Document: FSA-EXAMPLE v3, supplied draft dated 2026-10-15.
Parties: Cedar Lantern LLC (Customer) and Example Workshop LLC (Supplier).
This fictional text is source material for an evaluation, not real legal advice.

## 1. Services and acceptance

Supplier will deliver the website described in Schedule A by the date in that
schedule. Customer must reject a delivery in writing within two business days
or the delivery is deemed accepted. Schedule A is not included in this copy.

## 2. Fees

Customer must pay the full $18,000 fee before work starts. Fees are non-refundable
on termination, including when Supplier has not started the services.

## 3. Termination

Supplier may terminate immediately for convenience. Customer may terminate only
for a material breach that remains uncured for 30 days after written notice.

## 4. Liability

Supplier's total liability for any claim is limited to $100. Customer's liability
is unlimited. Neither party excludes liability for its own intentional misconduct.

## 5. Intellectual property

Supplier retains all rights in all work. Customer receives a non-transferable
license only while this Agreement remains in effect. The license ends immediately
when either party terminates, including a termination under section 3.

## 6. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the law of the State of New York. No forum is
specified. The supplied text does not identify where the parties operate.
